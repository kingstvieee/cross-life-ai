# Project Non-Negotiables

1. **Guardian is visible.** Do not convert it into a generic chat interface.
2. **Guardian identity persists while underlying models may change.**
3. **AI models cannot self-authorize.**
4. **All privileged actions pass through the Trust Kernel.**
5. **Credentials never enter model context.**
6. **Audit history cannot be disabled by Guardian.**
7. **Emergency Safe Mode is independent of Guardian reasoning.**
8. **No unrestricted autonomy mode.**
9. **No silent permission expansion.**
10. **No destructive or financial production actions during MVP.**
11. **Simulation Mode ships before production execution.**
12. **Do not merge STAARWAARDD with DIGGITSTAAR or Milo.**
13. **Do not claim mocked features are complete.** Clearly label mocked, simulated and production-ready components.
