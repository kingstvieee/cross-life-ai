## Required gate per product
1. Physical/sample approval or explicit preorder decision.
2. Final product photography/render media uploaded.
3. Title, description, materials, fit and care language verified.
4. Variants, sizes, prices and SKUs checked.
5. Inventory entered OR preorder/backorder policy explicitly configured.
6. Shipping profile and fulfilment origin verified.
7. Payments tested.
8. Returns/exchange policy linked.
9. Conversational-commerce fields standardized: canonical product name, category, material, fit, audience, price, availability, size range, colorways, hero image, structured description.
10. Only then change status DRAFT → ACTIVE.

## Recommended launch sequence
Start with a small verified capsule, not all 30 products. The current catalog is a prepared design catalog, not a sale-ready inventory system.
