# Guardian Lifelike Treatment

The Guardian identity is fixed to the established original Black Guardian artwork at `assets/images/staarwardd/guardian-toronto.png`. The cinematic must restore the premium lifelike treatment that was previously closer to the intended direction without changing the Guardian’s face, skin tone, suit palette, wing type, or silhouette.

| Area | Required lifelike treatment | Prohibited treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Face and anatomy | Natural facial proportions, skin texture, subtle expression, physically coherent shoulders, torso, hands, and legs. | A new person, plastic-like skin, a mask-like face, distorted limbs, or a stationary pose. |
| Suit and aura | Reflective silver-and-gold materials react to Toronto streetlight, mist, and portal light; warm aura follows the body without hiding it. | Flat surfaces, a new suit palette, an opaque glow, or a white-person substitution. |
| Wings | Broad white feathers articulate with aerodynamic pressure, with restrained gold-ember edge energy and individual feather motion. | Translucent mechanical wings, unrelated wing types, or constant rigid wings. |
| Flight | Continuous forward propulsion with body pitch, wing angle, weight shift, speed parallax, and one readable bank/climb conversion. | Vertical jumps, reversals, decorative flips, somersaults, sudden stops, or static hovering before the center arrival. |
| Camera | Motivated tracking, crane, and controlled orbit movements that follow the Guardian’s physical path. | Random cuts, unmotivated camera spins, or camera movement that contradicts the flight path. |
