# Guardian Street Flight v3

The launch begins in motion. It is an original cinematic sequence built around readable speed, changing perspective, and escalating energy. The Guardian has no borrowed emblem, costume language, named pose, sound, or choreography from another work.

| Clip | Duration | Action | Camera and continuity |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Street traversal | 7 seconds | Frame one shows the Guardian’s boots moving forward above a Toronto street. The camera rises to show the full body as the Guardian threads safely above moving traffic and visible pedestrians, then banks toward the center. | A low forward-moving chase camera rises from shoe level to a three-quarter full-body tracking angle. Toronto streets, vehicles, pedestrians, wing movement, and the Guardian remain present continuously. |
| Center and shield recall | 8 seconds | The Guardian rises to an open center above the street, extends an original palm-and-arm recall gesture, and a distinct Aegis artifact races in from the skyline before locking into place. | The camera arcs upward with the Guardian. The artifact is visible approaching before it arrives, with a contained energy wake rather than a sudden appearance. |
| Power, portal, return, and worlds | 9 seconds | The Guardian floats in the center while the wings illuminate from root to tip. Separate hand-directed energy streams slowly form an actual portal, the Guardian flies through, returns from it with controlled ember fire, and opens seven dimensional rings while remaining in the air. | The camera tracks the passage and return continuously. The seven worlds appear as distinct circles of light only after the fiery return; no interactive portal grid appears before this beat. |

The optional original sound cue follows the same arc: quiet Toronto street ambience, rising flight wind, a shield-arrival sweep, a slow energy buildup, a portal release, then a restrained ember-and-world-opening tail. It is never autoplayed.
