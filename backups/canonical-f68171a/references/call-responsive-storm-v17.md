# Call-Responsive Storm v17

This is a presentation-only refinement of the approved six-second storm-thrust bridge. It keeps the Guardian identity, one-hand thrust, continuous ascent, wing-pressure behavior, and exit height exactly unchanged.

| Moment | Sky response |
|---|---|
| Before the reach | Heavy layered charcoal-blue cloud banks occupy the upper frame, with a faint warm-gold glow trapped beneath the cloud ceiling. |
| At the upward call | The clouds visibly compress toward the raised-hand direction, the sky takes on an uncanny charged color shift, and a focused lightning bolt strikes into the high cloud field above the route. |
| During the ascent | Secondary lightning veins travel laterally inside the dense ceiling while wind pressure opens a narrow passage for the Guardian’s climb. |
| Final hold | The storm settles into a vast charged canopy, leaving a clear high-altitude stage for the preserved grand summoning. |

The lightning is a response in the sky, not a beam from the Guardian. No portal, layout, cover art, music, dialogue, or character change is added.
