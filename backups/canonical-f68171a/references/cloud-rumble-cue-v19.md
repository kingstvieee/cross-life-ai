# Cloud-Rumble Cue v19

The cue is an original **six-second non-vocal storm sound layer** for the lower-lightning bridge only. It is optional, remains off by default, and never includes rain.

| Time | Sound behavior |
|---|---|
| 00:00–00:01.5 | Low distant cloud pressure begins, airy and wide, with no beat or melody. |
| 00:01.5–00:03.5 | A deep controlled thunder roll swells as the raised-hand call reaches the cloud field. |
| 00:03.5–00:04.5 | One concise bright lightning crack lands, followed by a broad low-frequency cloud response. |
| 00:04.5–00:06 | The rumble falls away into a soft charged-air tail, leaving space for the preserved summoning cue. |

The cue excludes rain, wind gusts, speech, melody, percussion, vocals, and any recognizable media-derived sound.
