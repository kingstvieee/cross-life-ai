# Guardian Search Flight and Portal Constellation v5

This revision preserves the successful Aegis arrival, luminous suit, extended cinematic scale, and original score. It changes the flight into an intentional **search** and replaces abstract ring effects with seven real portals arriving in the approved circular Hub order.

| Segment | Action and visual intent | Camera and continuity |
|---|---|---|
| Search street pass | The Guardian scans a Toronto avenue at low altitude, then banks around a building edge. Head and body direction visibly change as if evaluating locations; the flight path continues rather than holding one position. | Full-body external tracking alternates with a brief forward point-of-view that sees the next street corridor and the edge of the wings. |
| High/low search maneuvers | The Guardian climbs above a roofline, looks across the city, then drops through a safe wide gap between modern towers and turns into a new avenue. | Camera rises and drops with the trajectory. A point-of-view check appears only briefly before returning to the Guardian’s full body. |
| Aegis and portal passage | The existing Aegis recall remains. The Guardian’s slow power buildup produces the large layered portal and full passage. | Existing high-hover framing remains visible long enough to read the artifact’s approach and portal’s depth. |
| Seven portals, one by one | After the ember return, seven **actual dimensional portals** form sequentially around the floating Guardian: Creativity, Work, Home, Wellbeing, Relationships, Events, and Style. Each begins as a spark, gains a visible rim and inner surface, then remains open before the next appears. | The camera pulls back to reveal the exact canonical circular arrangement; interactive destination controls are delayed until every portal exists. |
