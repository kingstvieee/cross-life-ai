# Guardian Identity Lock

The sole approved Guardian visual anchor is `assets/images/staarwardd/guardian-toronto.png`. The uploaded white Guardian was explicitly rejected and must never be used as a cinematic reference, source asset, or generated variation.

| Locked attribute | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Person | Preserve the established Black adult male Guardian, including his face, short dark hair, medium-deep brown skin tone, and dignified expression. Do not substitute a white Guardian or a different person. |
| Armor | Preserve the established luminous silver armor with warm gold articulated trim, shoulder structure, chest sigil, and flowing pale cloth panels. Do not replace it with a new suit palette or silhouette. |
| Wings | Preserve the broad white-feather wings with gold-ember energy along the edges. Do not replace them with translucent, metallic, or differently colored wings. |
| Aura | Keep the existing warm gold energy treatment as an enhancement only; it may reflect on the armor but must not obscure or recolor the Guardian. |
| Scope of changes | Only flight trajectory, speed cues, camera movement, Toronto scenery, portal staging, and optional music/effects may be revised. |

Any generated reference, clip, or preview that fails this lock is rejected and must not be integrated into the native app.
