This is a six-second optional sound cue for the approved storm-thrust bridge. It preserves the existing cloud-rumble behavior and adds distinct electrical transients at the visible lower lightning strike.

| Time | Sound behavior |
|---|---|
| 00:00–00:01.5 | Low distant cloud pressure, no rain and no musical elements. |
| 00:01.5–00:03.3 | Deep controlled thunder swell rises beneath the Guardian’s upward call. |
| 00:03.3–00:03.8 | A bright primary lightning crack punctuates the visible lower strike. |
| 00:03.8–00:04.5 | Two short, quieter branching electrical shock snaps follow at different stereo positions. |
| 00:04.5–00:06 | Broad low cloud response decays into charged air, leaving space for the summoning cue. |

The cue contains no rain, wind gusts, speech, melody, rhythm, recognizable media-derived sounds, or excessive loudness.
