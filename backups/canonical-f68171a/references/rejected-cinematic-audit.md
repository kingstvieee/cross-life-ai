# Current Build Rejection: Corrective Audit

The physical Android review correctly identified that the current build still relies on a small set of static Guardian pose images translated or scaled by the UI. The visible result is a presentation *about* a Guardian rather than a character-led experience. The opening, Hub, portal transition, and portal screens also retain too much shared card-and-copy structure.

## Non-negotiable correction

The replacement must show action rather than explain it. Guardian motion must be driven by reusable character state, layered environmental effects, and action-specific pose changes. The seven approved Hub cover images remain unchanged, but will become framed gateway surfaces inside a spatial field. The local preview, safety boundaries, routes, entitlement non-claims, and original portal IDs remain unchanged.
