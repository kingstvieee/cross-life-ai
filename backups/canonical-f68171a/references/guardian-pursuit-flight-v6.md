# Guardian Toronto Pursuit Flight v6

The prior search render is rejected because its motion reads as a vertical jump, reversal, and decorative flip. This replacement treats the Guardian as a continuously propelled aerial traveler in an original near-future Toronto pursuit sequence. The desired effect is high-budget, theatrical, dramatic, innovative, and futuristic; it does not recreate a specific franchise’s shots, characters, vehicles, or visual signatures.
