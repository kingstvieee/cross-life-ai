# Guardian Full-Body Animation Blueprint

## Global definition

The Guardian is an original adult celestial guide with silver, gold, and midnight armor, warm gold-violet illumination, luminous wing-like energy accents, and no dialogue. The clips are portrait **9:16**, silent, and designed for native playback over the existing app’s optional user-controlled cues. They preserve the approved Guardian identity without referencing or reproducing any external character or franchise choreography.

| Clip | Duration | Runtime state | Environment | Camera | Intended action |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Flight | 6 seconds | `flying` | Original Toronto at dusk | Tracking arc, then soft push-in | The full body flies a physically readable curved route into the city field. Wing energy, cloth tails, torso, head, and legs all move continuously. |
| Hover | 4 seconds | `hover` | Celestial command field | Locked medium-full framing | The full body breathes, subtly shifts balance, moves cloth and wings, and maintains alert eye direction. |
| Summon | 5 seconds | `portalOpening` | Toronto command field at night | Slow forward dolly | The Guardian begins with separated hands, traces two original energy arcs toward a shared ring, then opens both arms outward as the ring brightens. |

## Clip execution notes

Each clip contains **one continuous action in one traversable scene**. The first frame keeps the whole body in view and the same character remains visible throughout. No dialogue or embedded music is generated; the app controls optional opening, flight, shield, portal, transition, and ambient audio separately. The native app maintains existing static image fallbacks for unavailable video or reduced-motion states.
